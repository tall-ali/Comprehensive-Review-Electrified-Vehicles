# Comprehensive Review of Electrified Vehicles (EVs, HEVs, PHEVs)

This repository contains the research materials and writing drafts for a journal-style
review paper prepared for the course **MCE 4391 – Basic Mechanical Engineering**.

The paper provides a comprehensive review of Electric Vehicles (EVs), Hybrid Electric
Vehicles (HEVs), and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs), covering their technologies,
energy systems, environmental impacts, economic considerations, policies, and future
directions.

---

## Project Scope

The review paper addresses the following major themes:

- Vehicle classification and powertrain architectures  
- Battery and energy storage systems  
- Electric motors and power electronics  
- Charging infrastructure and grid interaction  
- Energy efficiency and environmental impact  
- Economic analysis and market trends  
- Policies, regulations, and global adoption  
- **Challenges, future trends, and concluding perspectives**

---

## Author Contribution

This repository documents the work related to **Section 10: Challenges, Future Trends & Conclusion**.

Responsibilities include:
- Identifying technical, economic, environmental, and policy-related challenges
- Synthesizing future trends in electrified vehicle technologies
- Formulating the concluding section of the review paper

All content is developed through literature review, synthesis, and original academic writing.

---

## Methodology

- Literature sources are primarily peer-reviewed journal articles and review papers
  accessed through Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, and ScienceDirect.
- References are managed using **Mendeley**, following the **IEEE citation style**.
- Figures and tables are student-created or redrawn from literature with proper citation.
- Similarity checks are conducted using **Turnitin** in compliance with course guidelines.

---

## Tools Used
